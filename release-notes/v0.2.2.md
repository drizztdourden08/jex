# Jex v0.2.2

## New

- **The AI can look at games.** It reads a game's full store description page by page, and it
  looks at its screenshots when you use a cloud model that can see images.
- **The AI can browse the Steam store for you.** It opens searches and pages in the Store tab,
  reads them, takes screenshots, and clicks, types and scrolls like a person while you watch.
  Purchases, checkout, sign-in, payment pages and password fields are blocked, and the AI will
  ask you to do those steps yourself.
- **The AI manages your wishlist groups.** It creates, renames, deletes and reorders groups,
  adds and removes games, switches a group between manual and filter mode, and adds games to
  your Steam wishlist. The groups update live while it works.
- **The AI can change your Steam store preferences.** Ask it to hide mature content or ignore
  a tag, and it ticks the boxes on the Store Content Preferences page and saves. The rest of
  your account pages stay off-limits.
- **A quieter chat footer.** The model, effort, context and permission pickers are plain text
  that opens a menu when clicked. The divider line is gone, the model picker is always there
  (even before a model is active), and menus at the bottom of the window open upward.
- **Launch and install feedback.** Clicking Play or Install shows a spinner and a short status
  line ("Starting Steam…", "Running", "Installing…"). The line's space is always reserved, so
  the layout doesn't shift.
- **Collapsible filter panel** with a summary line, remembered per page.
- **Cloud models.** You set up cloud models in one catalog with a key per provider. The chat
  picker groups them the same way.
- The library sorts by last played by default.

## Fixes

- **The AI engine updates itself.** Installed AI engines now update when a release ships a
  newer one. Before, you kept the old engine and never got new AI tools.
- Video theater mode and the screenshot lightbox cover the whole window.
- Secrets are encrypted with the OS's async secure storage.
- Upgraded Electron to 45.
